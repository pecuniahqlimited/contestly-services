# ADR 0017: Brands are accounts; people act as them

Status: accepted (8 Nov 2026). Roadmap row 8 (step 4), for launch on 20 Nov. Builds on ADR 0001 (option A), ADR 0012 §4, ADR 0013 and ADR 0015.

## Context

One login holds a personal profile and can own or join brand profiles. A brand has its own name, handle, logo, wallet, corner and inbox, and a team that hosts on its behalf. Most of what a brand needs already exists for people: a handle in people search, a corner keyed by account, a ledger wallet with top-ups and withdrawals under ADR 0013's lock order, DMs keyed by account. The question is whether a brand is a new kind of thing every one of those systems learns about, or an account they already understand.

## Decision

1. **A brand is a `users` row with `is_brand`** (its *account*): handle, name, logo emoji, bio, wallet, corner, inbox, payout accounts. Nobody signs in as it: it has no identities and no email of its own, so every existing sign-in door stays shut to it. The brand-only data lives in **`brands`** (one row per brand, `brands.id` = the account's `users.id`): lane, logo (an uploaded image's key; amended 9 Nov 2026: it was a link. Amended 10 Nov 2026: a verified brand's new logo waits in `pending_logo_key` with the live one kept until an admin approves it or rejects it with a note the team sees, at `/admin/brands/logos` and `/admin/brands/:id/logo/approve|reject`, logged and named by the key the admin looked at; there's no image safety model, so this is what keeps a verified badge from vouching for an image nobody checked), site, socials, business email, registration number (CAC), whether it will give cash prizes, and the verification status. `parties.brand_id → brands.id` is ADR 0001's column; the public host is `coalesce(brand_id, created_by_user_id)`, still only in `host.ts`. `users.is_verified` on the brand's row stays the one flag `canOfferNonCashPrize()` reads.
   - *Against:* every people query must keep brand accounts out where they don't belong (boards, crews, Climb, contacts). They can't get there: a brand account never has a session, and acting as a brand is refused on every route that doesn't opt in (2). Brands are meant to be *in* people search and DMs, and are.
   - *Rejected:* a separate brand entity with its own wallet, corner owner and chat member columns: a second owner type in the ledger, corners, chat and payments, each a change to a money or safety path two weeks before launch, for no query we can name.
2. **The active profile is a header, `x-act-as: <brand id>`** (`ACT_AS_HEADER` in shared), sent per request; no header is the person. Routes opt in (`config: { actAs: <capability> }`); anything else called with the header is **400 `not_available_as_brand`** (deny by default: joining, the Climb, crews, profiles of yourself). An opted-in route resolves the actor with `actor(req)`: the brand's account when the person's role grants the route's capability, else **403 `brand_role_forbidden`** (or 404 `not_a_member`, never saying whether a brand exists to outsiders). A banned brand account is refused like a banned person. The inbox room takes the same as a join option (`actAs`).
3. **Roles and capabilities** are one table in `packages/shared/src/brands.ts` (`BRAND_ROLE_CAPABILITIES`, `brandRoleCan()`), never re-derived:

   | | view | inbox read | parties host / inbox write / fulfil | team, settings, all parties, wallet view and top-up | withdraw, payout account, hand over, delete |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | owner | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
   | admin | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | |
   | host | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | | |
   | viewer | ✓ | ✓ | | | |

   `isHostTeam()` (now async) is the party's creator, or for a brand party its owners, admins and hosts. A Host funds a cash prize from the brand wallet only up to the brand's **host spending limit** (`brands.host_spend_limit_kobo`, ₦0 by default, set by the owner and admins in settings, each change a `settings_changed` event with its from and to); above it, 403 `brand_role_forbidden` with `{ capability: "parties.fund", role: "host", limitKobo }`. Owners and admins fund up to the brand caps and party limits. Entry and goods parties spend nothing from the wallet, so the limit doesn't touch them. The wallet itself (balance, history, top-up) is admins' and up. (Amended 9 Nov 2026; the first build let hosts spend up to the party limits.)
4. **A brand always has exactly one owner.** The creator is the owner; ownership moves only by hand-over (`POST /brands/:id/owner`, owner only, to an existing member, who becomes owner while the old owner becomes admin), in one transaction with the brand row locked. The owner can't leave or be removed; they hand over or delete the brand. An owner can't delete their personal account while they own a live brand (`owns_brand`). Every team change (invite, accept, decline, revoke, role change, remove, leave, hand-over) is a `brand_events` row, append-only, with who and what.
5. **Invites** go to a handle (an existing account) or an email (anyone). An email invite is matched on the lowercased email when an account with that verified email exists, later signs up with it or adds it (`claimEmailInvites` in `becomeMember` and email add), and then waits for that person's accept or decline like any invite: being sent an email doesn't put you in a team. The invite email is sent from the request through `EmailSender`, like a sign-in code. One open invite per person or email per brand; the team size is config (`brands.maxTeam`, default 20).
6. **Hosting as a brand.** `POST /parties` with the header creates a party whose `created_by_user_id` is the person and `brand_id` the brand. Brands never play: the creator isn't seated in a sealed brand party and a brand account can't take a seat; the team's curate lockout is ADR 0003's, now over the whole host team. A host's cash prize is held from **the brand's** cash, and every later money step (settlement's host cut, the held-prize return, a cancel's release) uses the party's funding account, `publicHostId(party)`. Nobody on a brand's team, any role, takes a seat in the brand's parties with a prize of any kind (an entry, a cash prize or goods: `partyHasPrize()`), 403 `brand_team_member`, checked in `takeSeat()` so every seat path has it; the brand's free parties are fine, and there a seated team member is a player who may leave. Settlement checks again (as does a rejected held prize's pass-on), so someone who joined the team after taking a seat is skipped like a banned player and the prize goes to the next place. (Amended 9 Nov 2026; the first build let team members play and win the brand's sealed parties.)
7. **Verification.** After name and logo, a checklist: CAC number (`RC` or `BN` and digits), a logo, a site or social link, the business email verified by a 6-digit code (the sign-in code machinery, a purpose bound to the brand), and a payout account only if the brand says it will give cash prizes. `POST /brands/:id/verification/submit` (admin and up) moves `draft | changes_needed → in_review` only with the checklist complete; admins approve or ask for changes with a note (`/admin/brands/:id/approve`, `/request-changes`, logged, with the reason). Approval sets `is_verified`, calls `createBrandCorner()` in the same transaction, and queues the `verification_update` nudge to the owner and admins; changes needed does the same with the note. An unverified brand hosts **free parties only** (`brand_not_verified`); approval is what unlocks entry fees, cash prizes and goods. Editing a checked field of a verified brand doesn't unverify it; admins can ask for changes on a verified brand, which unverifies it until re-approved (its corner stays, with its follows).
8. **The brand wallet** is the brand account's ledger accounts: funded by top-up (`POST /wallet/topups` acting as the brand), spent on cash prizes, credited with host cuts. Withdrawals and payout accounts are owner-only; a brand withdrawal needs the brand verified and **its owner** to pass a person's withdrawal checks (verified email and phone, not frozen), so a payout still reaches one real person (`withdrawBlockReason`'s `brand_unverified`, `owner_cannot_withdraw`). Everything else in ADR 0013 (debit first, two admins, holds, owed, lock order with the brand's `users` row first) is unchanged, because the brand is an account.
9. **Fulfilment.** Goods are `prize_fulfilments` with the brand as `host_user_id`. The brand's team (host and up) marks a prize *arranged*, then *delivered*; the winner can *dispute* an arranged or delivered prize with a note; each step is stamped and is a `prize_fulfilment_events` row. `prize_ready` goes to the winner and the brand's hosts when arranged; `prize_delivery_due` to both on day 3 and day 7 after it was awarded while it isn't delivered (the worker's hourly sweep, once each). A person host's goods prize (none today; goods need a verified brand) would follow the same path.
10. **Messages from the brand.** A DM from a brand comes from the brand's account (`dmPermission()`'s brand rule unchanged: only people who joined its parties, never cold, never a request); `chat_messages.sent_by_user_id` keeps which team member wrote it, for moderation, never shown. Owners, admins and hosts write; viewers read.
11. **Search and corners.** People search returns brands with `isBrand`; a brand's corner exists only by approval, so suggested corners show brands once verified. A brand never earns a corner through the unlock thresholds. Its team runs it by role, acting as the brand (amended 9 Nov 2026): owners and admins edit it, mute and delete posts and replies (`corner.manage`), hosts post (`corner.post`), viewers read; the corner page says what the viewer can do (`viewer.can`, with their `brandRole`), and nobody on the team follows the brand's corner (`cannot_follow_own_corner`), acting or not.
12. **Curate caps for brands** (ADR 0015): `curateHostType()` returns `brand` for a verified brand. Migration 0058 writes a new party-template config version raising the brand caps to entry ₦5,000, host prize ₦500,000, pot ₦500,000 (the party limits' ceilings: a verified brand's curate party is bounded by the same limits as its sealed ones). The share cap and settlement checks stay one set for everyone.

## Consequences

- `isHostTeam()` and `hostTeamOf()` read `brand_members`; every caller awaits.
- Deleting a brand (owner) soft-deletes its account (ADR 0010's rules: refused with cash in it or an open party), archives its corner and ends the team. Banning a brand account bans the brand; banning a person removes nothing from their brands but shuts every door to acting as them.
- Admins get a brands list, the review queue, team views and the brand wallet in the existing per-account ledger view.

## Not decided here

- Brand templates (product knowledge, launch, the feedback poll): after launch.
- A spending limit per host (today one limit covers every Host on the brand).
- Changing a brand's business email after verification (needs a re-check; refused for now: `brand_email_locked`).
