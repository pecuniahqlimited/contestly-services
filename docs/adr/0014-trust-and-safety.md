# ADR 0014: Trust and safety: block, mute, report, holds, moderation, ban

Status: accepted (5 Nov 2026). Roadmap row 11. Required for launch (Apple: block and report for user-generated content).

## Context

People meet strangers in public parties, write to each other, post in corners and name things (handles, parties, crews, corners). We need a person to be able to shut someone out on their own, a way to tell us, a queue where we act on it, and a ban that actually holds money still. Most of the pieces already exist in part: `moderation_items` (voice transcripts, corner reply reports), `bannedAt` with `assertNotBanned()` at every door, `isWalletFrozen()` in `payable.ts`, corner mutes, a profanity list.

## Decision

1. **Block** (`POST/DELETE /users/:id/block`, `GET /me/blocks`) is one row (`blocks`: blocker, blocked) whose effect is **mutual and silent**: every rule asks "has either blocked the other" (`blockedEitherWay()`, `notBlockedWith()` in `packages/db/src/safety/`), and the blocked person is never told; what they hit is the same refusal as any other (`cannot_message`, `cannot_invite`, `cannot_challenge`, `not_found`). Between the two:
   - `dmPermission()` takes a `blocked` fact and refuses (so no DM, no request, no Challenge, which already asks it); their DM thread drops out of both people's Chats;
   - mentions of each other aren't resolved (`resolveMentions(text, senderId)`), so no tag and no mention nudge;
   - `connectedTo()` (party and crew invites) and follower asks leave them out; pending party and crew invites between them are dismissed at the block;
   - `yourPeople()` leaves them out, and with it mutuals, the friends board, the feed, the lobby's "who's in" and win-back copy; `/me/contacts` and suggested corners too;
   - the blocked person can't react or reply on the blocker's corner posts (`not_found`), and each side stops seeing the other's replies;
   - `nudge()` records nothing about one for the other: every nudge names who it's about (`about`, the actor included) and is skipped (`blocked`) when any of them is blocked either way with the recipient.
   They can still be in the same public party (we don't police seats). There, each one's party chat lines are filtered out **per recipient on the server**: rooms keep each member's hidden authors and send a line only to the clients allowed to see it; history (`GET /chats/:id/messages`) filters for the viewer. Matchmaking doesn't exist yet (row 7); `blockedPairsAmong()` is the hook it must call. Brands are users, so a block covers brand profiles when row 8 lands with no change.
2. **Mute** (`POST/DELETE /users/:id/mute`, `person_mutes`) is one-way and only hides: their chat lines (party, DM, crew, corner replies) are hidden for you, and no nudge about them reaches you (`muted`). Nothing else changes. Corner mutes (host-side, `corner_mutes`) stay as they are: separate table, separate meaning.
3. **Report** is one endpoint (`POST /reports { target: { type, id }, reason, note? }`) for a user, a chat line (the queue files it as party, DM or crew by its thread; a voice line as its voice note), a corner post or reply (`POST /corner-replies/:id/report` is now a thin alias), a party (title or cover) and a voice note. Reasons are fixed (`REPORT_REASONS`; `other` needs text). The reporter must be able to see what they report. Each target becomes one `moderation_items` row (the existing one per kind and ref), with a **snapshot taken at the first report** (text, author, thread, title, cover, transcript), so a deleted message can still be reviewed; each reporter is one `reports` row (a repeat by the same person is `replayed`). The response carries `offerBlock` (the person behind it, unless that's you or you've blocked them already) for the app's "Block them too?". Limited per user (`RATE_LIMIT_REPORTS_PER_HOUR`). Question flags stay their own thing.
4. **Holds.** When distinct reporters on one item reach `safety.holdReporters` (config; the schema's minimum is the floor), its content is **hidden for everyone** pending review (`held_at`; how depends on the target: the line, post or reply gets `hidden_at`; a party's title and cover are swapped for neutral ones, the originals kept in the snapshot). When distinct people reporting one person's things within `userReportWindowHours` reach `userTimeoutReporters`, that person gets a **chat timeout** for `autoTimeoutHours`. Both are logged as the system (`moderation.auto_hold`, `user.auto_chat_timeout`), raise a warning alert (`safety.content_held`, `safety.user_timed_out`), and are reversed by an admin (release the hold; lift the timeout).
5. **The queue** (`/admin/moderation`): filter by type, reason and status (open, actioned, dismissed), most-reported first. An item shows its snapshot, reporter count and reasons, the surrounding chat lines (five either side), the subject's history (their other items and the admin actions on them), and for a voice note its transcript beside a signed audio link (the transcriber stays a mock; `Transcriber` is the hook). Actions, each with a reason and logged: **dismiss** (releases a hold), **remove** (hides it for good), **warn** (a notice the person sees next time the app opens: `GET /me/notices`), **chat timeout** for hours (no sending in party chat, DMs, crews or corner replies: `chat_timeout`), **suspend** for hours, **ban**. Reporters get a notice that says "action taken" or "reviewed", never what.
6. **Ban and the wallet.** `isWalletFrozen()` (the one freeze check) stays "banned", now counting suspensions; it is asked by withdrawals (request and send, unchanged), and newly by top-up creation and taking a seat, so a ban freezes withdrawals, entries and top-ups even if a door is missed. Cash stays where it is; nothing is confiscated, nothing touches the ledger internals. A ban (or suspension) queues the worker's `ban_effects`: the person's hosted **open** parties are cancelled through `adminCancelParty()` → `refundAndCancel()`; their seats in others' open parties are freed and refunded as paid (`removeSeat()`, the leave's refund without the leave's time lock); their `requested` withdrawals are **held** for a payments admin (`awaiting_approval`, `blocked:frozen`), never reversed. Live parties are left to the existing rules (rooms drop them, settlement skips them). **Suspend** is a ban with `banned_until`; it counts as a ban until then and the worker's minute sweep lifts it (logged as the system). Unban and unsuspend unfreeze; a payments admin's ban or suspension still needs a second admin and gives no permissions back (ADR 0013). A banned person's every refusal is `403 account_banned` with `details: { category, until }`; the app shows a plain screen with that and "Contact support" from `GET /config`.
7. **Names.** One filter (`checkName()` in `packages/shared/src/nameFilter.ts`) for handles, display names, bios, party titles, corner names and bios, and crew names: slurs are refused (`422 name_not_allowed`), milder words masked (handles refuse both, since a mask can't be a handle). It normalises case, accents and look-alike digits. The built-in list is small and deliberately conservative (English slurs, and Nigerian terms we could source with confidence: ethnic slurs such as *nyamiri* and *ofe mmanu*; insults such as *ashawo*, *omo ale*, *shege*, *dan iska* refused; *mumu*, *olodo*, *werey*, *oloshi* masked). Words that are also surnames or everyday words (*gambari*, *aboki*) are left out so real names aren't refused. Ops extend it without a release through `safety.blockedTerms` and `safety.maskedTerms`; it needs a native-speaker review before launch.

## What a ban did before this, and what this adds

Before: `assertNotBanned()` at API sessions, sign-in token minting and every room's `onAuth`; rooms drop the user on `admin:events`; settlement skips them for prizes; withdrawals refused at request and held at send (`frozen`); payments permissions removed; payments admins need a second admin; their corner hidden; nudges skipped. Added: suspension (timed ban, auto-lift), the category and expiry on the banned screen, top-up and seat freeze checks, hosted open parties cancelled and refunded, seats in open parties refunded, requested withdrawals held at once.

## Consequences

- Hiding is per target (a registry in `packages/db/src/safety/targets.ts`: load a snapshot, hide, show), so a new reportable thing is one entry.
- Per-recipient filtering costs one set lookup per line per client; the sets are cached per room process and refreshed on `safety:events` when a block or mute changes.
- Holds are blunt (a count), easy to explain and reverse. A share-of-room rule (like question flags) can come later for big parties.
